## 二、自编入门示范（2 篇，水平约 1000–1500 词）

> 标注【自编】，非真题，用于 P1–P2 起步热身。

### 示范 1：At the Coffee Shop（8 空）

Every morning, I stop at the coffee shop near my home. The barista, Mike, always remembers my **1** ____. "The usual?" he asks with a smile. I nod and hand him two dollars. He makes a cup of coffee and **2** ____ it to me. "Anything else today?" he says. "Yes, a croissant, **3** ____," I answer. I take my coffee and sit by the window. Ten minutes later, I am ready for the day. It is a small **4** ____, but it makes my morning **5** ____. I think I will come here **6** ____ tomorrow. Good coffee, good **7** ____, and a good start — what **8** ____ do I need?

1. A. name　B. order　C. money　D. seat
2. A. gives　B. sells　C. shows　D. throws
3. A. too　B. also　C. either　D. again
4. A. habit　B. shop　C. job　D. place
5. A. better　B. worse　C. later　D. earlier
6. A. again　B. before　C. never　D. once
7. A. coffee　B. tea　C. milk　D. water
8. A. more　B. less　C. else　D. other

**答案与解析：**
1. **B**（order：点的单；Mike 记得"我"点的东西）
2. **A**（gives：做好递给我；sells 要收钱、shows 是展示，都不如 gives 顺）
3. **A**（too：也，放句尾；also 不放句尾、either 用于否定）
4. **A**（habit：习惯；"这是个小习惯"呼应全文）
5. **A**（better：让早晨更好）
6. **A**（again：明天再来一次）
7. **A**（coffee：呼应开头）
8. **C**（else：还需要什么别的；what else 固定搭配）

### 示范 2：My Weekend（8 空）

My name is Anna. I live in a small city with my parents and my dog, Max. On weekends, I like to **1** ____ up late and make pancakes for the family. My father **2** ____ coffee, and my mother reads the newspaper. After breakfast, Max and I go to the park. He runs **3** ____ and plays with other dogs. I sit on a bench and **4** ____ people. In the afternoon, I practice English for about one hour. I listen to a short story and **5** ____ the new words. In the evening, we watch a movie together. It is a simple life, but I am **6** ____ with it. I want to **7** ____ English well so I can travel and **8** ____ new friends around the world.

1. A. get　B. wake　C. stand　D. stay
2. A. drinks　B. eats　C. cooks　D. washes
3. A. fast　B. slowly　C. quietly　D. carefully
4. A. watch　B. hear　C. help　D. join
5. A. remember　B. forget　C. lose　D. repeat
6. A. happy　B. angry　C. sad　D. busy
7. A. learn　B. teach　C. read　D. speak
8. A. make　B. see　C. meet　D. know

**答案与解析：**
1. **B**（wake up late：睡到自然醒；get up 也可以，但这里只给了 wake）
2. **A**（drinks coffee：喝咖啡；爸爸不会 eat/wash 咖啡）
3. **A**（fast：跑得快；和后面 plays 并列，选最自然的副词）
4. **A**（watch people：看路人；hear 是听、join 是加入）
5. **A**（remember：记住生词；forget/lose 意思反了）
6. **A**（happy with：对…满意，固定搭配）
7. **A**（learn English well：学好英语）
8. **C**（meet new friends：交新朋友；make friends 也对，但选项是 meet）

## 三、做完一篇后的收尾（5 分钟）

1. 把错的题抄进飞书打卡表「备注」：错因是"词义不清 / 固定搭配 / 上下文没看懂"哪一类
2. 把短文大声朗读 2 遍（练语感，顺便练音标）
3. 摘 3 个生词进欧路词典生词本（正好用上词表素材）
